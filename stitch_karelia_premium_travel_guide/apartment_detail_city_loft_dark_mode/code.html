Architectural Sophistication

Designed with the rigorous precision of Nordic modernism, the Petrozavodsk City Loft offers a seamless blend of industrial heritage and contemporary luxury. Featuring four-meter soaring ceilings and a southern-facing glass curtain wall, the space is flooded with natural light throughout the long northern days.

Every material has been selected to reflect the Karelian landscape: locally sourced light oak, polished concrete that mirrors the volcanic origins of the region, and custom teal accents that echo the depth of Lake Onega.
