The Obsidian Retreat: Saimaa Lakehouse

Perched on the edge of the volcanic rock formations of the Saimaa archipelago, the Obsidian Retreat offers an unparalleled connection to the wild Karelian landscape. Designed by visionary Nordic architects, the structure disappears into the forest canopy, utilizing charred timber and expansive glass to blur the lines between interior comfort and raw nature.

The Experience

Every morning begins with the silent dance of mist over the lake. Our guests are provided with exclusive access to the surrounding 400-hectare private reserve, featuring hidden hiking trails and secluded spots for aurora viewing. The interior features heated basalt floors and custom-made furniture from local artisans, ensuring that even the coldest nights are met with absolute warmth and luxury.
