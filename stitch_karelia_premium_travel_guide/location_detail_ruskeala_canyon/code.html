The Jewel of the North

Ruskeala Canyon is a former marble quarry that has been transformed into one of Karelia's most mesmerizing natural attractions. Once the source of stone for St. Petersburg's grandest cathedrals and palaces, the quarry was flooded after the industrial operations ceased, creating a deep, crystal-clear reservoir enclosed by sheer white marble walls.

The water's clarity allows visitors to see deep into the flooded shafts, where the play of light against the marble creates ethereal shades of turquoise and emerald. The surrounding mountain park features a network of well-maintained hiking trails that offer panoramic views of the canyon from various vantage points.

Adventurous travelers can explore the canyon via rowing boats or take an underground tour to see the frozen marble caves and tunnels that remain from the quarry's mining era. During winter, the canyon transforms into a silent, frozen cathedral of ice and snow, often illuminated by artistic light installations.
